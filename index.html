How to Play

Offense. Pick a formation. Your QB walks to each receiver: tap a direction to give him a route. → streak · ← 5-yard hook · ↑ / ↓ 6-yard out or in toward that side of the field. Tap the button to hike. At the line each receiver gets an arrow (highest = ↑, middle = →, lowest = ↓) that stays with him all play. To throw, hold the button and tap his arrow, or just tap the button to throw to the most open receiver. The QB can run any time, but can't pass once he crosses the line.

Defense. Your linebacker walks to each defensive back: tap the route you think his receiver will run, in the direction it will move on screen (the computer drives left, so ← is the streak and → the hook). Guess right and he blankets that receiver. After the snap you steer the linebacker; press the button while running to dive.

Running. The QB can run any time, but once he crosses the line he can't pass. After a catch or an interception the computer keeps everyone moving until you press a direction; then you take over the ball carrier, or the player closest to him. A ball carrier can dive too: if an opponent touches him during the dive he's down where he lands; if nobody touches him before he's up, he keeps running. Teammates of the ball carrier try to get in front of him to block.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space = button, 1–4 = formation.
